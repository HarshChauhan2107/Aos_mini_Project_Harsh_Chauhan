Six ways to schedule disk I/O

Explore the policy behind each simulation.

06 ALGORITHMS
01

FCFS

Services requests in the exact order in which they arrive. Simple and fair, but can cause large head movements.

TIME · O(n)
02

SSTF

Selects the pending request closest to the current head position. Often reduces movement but may cause starvation.

TIME · O(n²)
03

SCAN

The head travels in one direction to the disk boundary, then reverses to service requests on the other side.

TIME · O(n log n)
04

C-SCAN

Services requests in one direction only, then returns to the opposite end to begin another sweep.

TIME · O(n log n)
05

LOOK

Works like SCAN but reverses at the last pending request instead of travelling to the physical disk boundary.

TIME · O(n log n)
06

C-LOOK

Works like C-SCAN but jumps between the furthest pending requests rather than visiting disk boundaries.

TIME · O(n log n)
